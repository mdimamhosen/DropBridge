AirDrop for Android and every computer

Send photos, videos, folders, huge files and text between your phone and your Mac, Windows or Linux PC. Straight over your Wi-Fi, encrypted, with no account and no cloud.

Three steps. That's it.

No cables, no pairing menus, no uploading.

1

Open it on both

Same Wi-Fi (or your phone's hotspot). Devices appear on their own in about a second.

2

Pick & tap

Drop files on a device on your computer, or choose files, photos, a folder or text on your phone. Also works from any app's Share menu.

3

Accept

The other device asks first. New device? Check both screens show the same 6-digit code. Trust it once and skip that next time.

Built for big, real-world transfers

Everything you expect, nothing you have to configure.

Any size

Streams instead of loading files into memory. Tested with 10 GB files at flat ~40 MB memory.

Whole folders

Keeps folder structure, empty folders, hidden files, Unicode names and modification times.

Never half a file

Every file is checked with BLAKE3 before it appears. Unfinished data stays hidden.

Survives Wi-Fi drops

Reconnects automatically and continues from the last verified byte. Pause, cancel, or retry only what failed.

Text & links too

Send a link or note to the other device and copy it with one click.

Fast

The engine moves 1.5–2 GB/s in tests, so your Wi-Fi is the only limit. See measurements.

Private by design

Your files go from one device to the other. Nowhere else.

End-to-end encrypted

Every connection uses TLS 1.3 with a unique key per device, stored in your OS keychain.

No middleman

Matching codes or a QR scan prove you're talking to the right device, not someone on the same network.

You decide

Nothing is saved without your approval. Block or forget devices any time.

No tracking

No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Read the threat model.

Questions

My devices don't see each other

Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi (guest networks often block this) and DropBridge is open on both. On a Mac allow "Local Network" access. You can always pair with the QR code under Pair a device.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Turn on your phone's hotspot and join it from your computer. No internet is needed either way.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open source under Apache-2.0, no ads, no account.

Why does macOS / Windows warn me on first launch?

Early builds aren't code-signed yet. Right-click → Open on macOS, or "More info → Run anyway" on Windows. Compare the file with SHA256SUMS.txt if you want to be sure.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

No cables, no pairing menus, no uploading to someone else's server.

1

Open it on both

Same Wi-Fi, or your phone's hotspot. Devices find each other in about a second.

2

Pick & tap

Drop files on a device on your computer. On Android choose files, photos, a folder or text — or use Share from any app.

3

Accept

The other side asks first. New device? Both screens show the same 6-digit code. Trust it once, skip it next time.

FAQ

Questions

My devices don't see each other

Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi (guest networks often block this) and DropBridge is open on both. On a Mac allow “Local Network” access. You can always pair with the QR code under “Pair a device”.

Does it need internet?

No. Devices talk directly. No Wi-Fi router? Turn on your phone's hotspot and join it from the computer.

Is it faster than AirDrop?

We don't claim that yet. The engine adds little over a plain disk copy, so your Wi-Fi decides the speed. Real-device comparisons are being collected — you can submit yours.

Why does macOS / Windows warn on first launch?

Early builds aren't code-signed yet. Right-click → Open on macOS, or More info → Run anyway on Windows. Compare the file with SHA256SUMS.txt if you want to be sure.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open source under Apache-2.0. No ads, no account, no tracking.
